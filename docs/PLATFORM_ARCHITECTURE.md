# Edward D. Clark Platform Architecture

## Purpose

EdwardDClark.com is the canonical public identity for Edward D. Clark. It should explain who he is, what he is researching, how his work is evolving, and where visitors can find authoritative scholarly records and applied initiatives.

The site is not intended to duplicate LinkedIn, reproduce the Collective-State Inference research platform, or become a general-purpose blog. Its role is to provide the coherent front door to the complete professional and research ecosystem.

## Core narrative

Every page should reinforce one central idea:

> Edward D. Clark is developing a new way for artificial intelligence to understand collectives.

Supporting dimensions include enterprise architecture, independent research, artificial intelligence, organizational systems, entrepreneurship, and applied innovation.

## Brand hierarchy

```text
Edward D. Clark
├── Personal identity: EdwardDClark.com
├── Research program: Collective-State Inference
│   ├── Papers
│   ├── Reference materials
│   ├── Methodology
│   ├── Observatory
│   └── Research artifacts
└── Applied innovation
    └── Vibe Village
```

Edward D. Clark is the parent identity. Collective-State Inference is the principal research program. Vibe Village is an entrepreneurial and applied-innovation initiative.

## Primary audiences

1. Researchers, reviewers, and prospective academic collaborators
2. Enterprise architecture and AI leaders
3. Conference organizers, journalists, and podcast hosts
4. Prospective partners and collaborators
5. Visitors arriving from LinkedIn, ORCID, Google Scholar, GitHub, or CSI

## Site map

### Home

**Question answered:** What important problem is Edward D. Clark pursuing?

The homepage remains concise and research-forward. It should lead with the central collective-level inference question, introduce the three dimensions of current work, and route visitors to Research and About.

### About

**Question answered:** Who is Edward D. Clark, and why is he positioned to pursue this research?

Planned sections:

- Portrait-led introduction
- Professional journey
- Research philosophy
- Areas of practice
- Education
- Scholarly identity
- Short, medium, and extended biographies

### Research

**Question answered:** What is the research agenda?

This page is the personal-site map of the research program. It should summarize theory, operationalization, validation, and the relationship to the CSI platform without duplicating the complete research record.

### Publications

**Question answered:** What scholarly outputs are attributable to Edward D. Clark?

Each output should support consistent metadata:

- Title
- Publication type
- Status
- Abstract
- Citation
- DOI, when available
- PDF or canonical record
- BibTeX
- RIS
- Supplemental material
- Related research

The CSI platform may remain the authoritative source for CSI-specific artifacts, while EdwardDClark.com provides the author-level publication index.

### Current Work

**Question answered:** What is active now?

A curated status page, not a changelog. It may include current papers, Observatory status, MBA progress, selected architecture work, and active ventures. Content should be dated and intentionally maintained.

### Speaking

**Question answered:** What can Edward speak about, and how can organizers evaluate fit?

Planned elements:

- Speaking topics
- Short biography
- Portrait
- Selected talks or appearances
- Downloadable speaker assets
- Inquiry route

### Media

**Question answered:** What approved assets and descriptions may others use?

Planned assets:

- Portraits
- Short, medium, and long biographies
- EDC mark
- Approved CSI description
- Publication list
- Scholarly identifiers
- Contact guidance

### Contact

**Question answered:** Why is the visitor reaching out?

Contact routes should be purpose-specific:

- Research collaboration
- Speaking
- Media
- Professional inquiry

### Ideas — later phase

A curated public research notebook for concise conceptual notes, hypotheses, and methodological observations. This is not a chronological blog and should not dilute formal scholarly outputs.

## Navigation model

### Primary navigation

```text
Home | About | Research | Publications | Current Work
```

### Secondary navigation

```text
Speaking | Media | Contact
```

The initial implementation may introduce these pages progressively, but the final navigation should remain stable and avoid constant restructuring.

## Success criteria

A first-time visitor should understand within ten seconds:

1. Edward D. Clark is an independent researcher and enterprise architect.
2. His distinctive research concerns AI inference of collective-level states.
3. Collective-State Inference is the named research program.
4. The site provides clear routes to the full research record and professional identity.

The platform should feel authoritative, current, restrained, and durable rather than promotional or résumé-driven.
