Collective-State Inference: A Multilevel Computational Framework for Collective Awareness in Artificial Intelligence

The foundational manuscript develops the theoretical conditions under which computational evidence warrants interpretation as a latent state of a bounded collective, including construct boundaries, cross-level composition, validation, uncertainty, and responsible use.

Submitted: 11 August 2026 to Computational and Mathematical Organization Theory. Current stage: Technical check. The manuscript has not yet entered peer review.
