Featured manuscript
In peer reviewTheoretical research manuscript · Edward D. Clark

Collective-State Inference: A Computational Organization Theory for Collective-Aware Artificial Intelligence

When does evidence about interacting participants justify an AI system's claim about the condition of a collective? This manuscript proposes the theoretical requirements for that interpretation, including collective boundaries, how evidence combines, uncertainty, and comparison with simpler explanations.

Status: Unpublished and currently in external journal peer review following editorial suitability assessment. It has not been accepted, and CSI has not yet been empirically validated.

CSI Reference, Edition 0.1

A fixed edition of the program's conceptual foundations, including ten reference articles, construct boundaries, methodological principles, scholarly sources, and editorial policies. The archive provides a persistent record alongside the evolving online Reference.

Archival publication establishes citability and traceability; it does not establish peer review or empirical validation.

Conceptual illustration
Synthetic illustrationPublicly available

CSI in a collaborative listening session

A worked example follows a hypothetical listening group from observable participation to a candidate estimate of collective engagement. It shows why the distribution, reciprocity, and persistence of interaction may matter alongside activity totals.

The data are entirely synthetic. This is a conceptual illustration, not an implemented or empirically validated CSI system.

Research infrastructure

Tools supporting the research process.

Private prototype · v0.1

CSI MCP

An experimental machine-readable interface for retrieving approved CSI resources, checking proposed observation sets, and constructing traceable evidence ledgers. It supports preparation for research; it does not estimate or score collective states.

Availability: Public overview; private development and evaluation. The endpoint and source repository are not publicly available.

Exploratory instrumentation

CSI Research Observatory

A public prototype showing how delayed, aggregate website interaction observations become audience-pattern indicators and candidate evidence.

Website visitors are not assumed to form a qualified bounded collective. The Observatory does not publish validated CSI collective-state estimates.
