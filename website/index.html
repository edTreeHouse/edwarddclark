Can AI inferthe state of acollective?

Modern AI has become remarkably good at modeling individuals. My research examines how AI can infer latent conditions that emerge within bounded collectives—and what is required for those inferences to be theoretically valid, empirically supported, and responsibly used.

Research

Collective-State Inference

Collective-State Inference is a research program for understanding latent, emergent conditions of bounded collectives—such as engagement, cohesion, conflict, and alignment—from observable interaction patterns.

The work examines a central limitation in contemporary AI: understanding a group is not always reducible to understanding each member separately.
